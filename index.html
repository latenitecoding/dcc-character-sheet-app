ACTIONS
AttackUse a Skill, Spell, or other effect that requires an Attack Skill Check against a target, rolling damage if Successful. Drawing or swapping a weapon from a Hotlist is free as part of that Action. • MoveMove up to your crawler’s Move value (default 20 feet). • HelpAid another crawler with their non-combat Skill Check, adding a 1d2 (without Skill Ranks) or 1d6 (with Skill Ranks) before the roll. • Use a Hotlist ItemUse a prepared consumable item or ready another Hotlist Item. • Make a CheckMake a Skill Check or Stat Check to do what you want to do in the scene.
Cast a SpellChoose a Spell or scroll in your Hotlist, and roll a Spell Check if it’s an Attack. If it’s a non-scroll Spell, spend Mana to cast it. • RetrieveMove an item from a crawler’s Inventory to their Hotlist or hand. Requires only a Step to pick something up off the floor. • Call a PlayPlan an Action, giving an ally a bonus when they perform it. Grants a 2d6 bonus. • Look for CluesIn a Boss encounter, gain information about a foe or environment. This is also a Make a Check type Action.
